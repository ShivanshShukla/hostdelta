# Code of Conduct

HostDelta welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.
This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project-managed community spaces.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Critique ideas rather than people.
Welcome questions, explain assumptions, and give actionable feedback. Respect
privacy and the operational constraints of people sharing infrastructure reports.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on
[haramj's GitHub profile](https://github.com/haramj). If no private contact option
is available, open a minimal issue asking for a private contact route; do not name
people or disclose incident details publicly. Security vulnerabilities have a
separate private reporting route described in SECURITY.md.

The maintainer will review reports in context and avoid sharing identifying details
unnecessarily. Responses may include requesting edits, removing content, issuing
a warning, or temporarily or permanently restricting participation. Serious abuse
may result in immediate removal. Anyone affected may request reconsideration
through the same private contact route.
